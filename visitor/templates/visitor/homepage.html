About Us

Rooted in Mauritius, serving local and international clients

Abhasa Financial Services has a team of professionals dedicated to serving clients by ensuring that all regulatory obligations are met, and by offering guidance on the financial aspects of their businesses. Our clients are mainly family-owned enterprises operating in a wide range of activities. We have gained valuable experience advising both foreign and Mauritian nationals on setting up businesses in the domestic market, from incorporation and bank account opening to setting up the accounting framework.
